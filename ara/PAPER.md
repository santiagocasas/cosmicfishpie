# CosmicFishPie JURECA Operations

Agent-native record for scheduler configuration, environments, scaling experiments, and reproducible sampling runs.

This artifact also carries a second, merged track (all IDs prefixed `V-`) recording
CAMB/CLASS paper-validation workflow decisions and heuristics from a parallel workstation
session. The two tracks are not cross-referenced against each other.

## Layers

- `logic/`: crystallized research knowledge
- `src/`: implementation and environment references
- `trace/`: decisions and experiments
- `evidence/`: validation and benchmark evidence
- `staging/`: observations awaiting closure
